Making Every Indian Citizen Space-Aware

Our Vision

From bicycle-carried rocket parts in Thumba to soft-landing near the lunar South Pole and observing the solar corona from the L1 Lagrange point, India's space program is a story of determination, scientific brilliance, and self-reliance.

Space Bharat bridges the gap between complex orbital mechanics and every Indian citizen. By delivering spoken stories and voice-enabled conversational AI in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, English, and more, we ensure language is never a barrier to space awareness.

Technology & Voice Stack

- Speech Synthesis (TTS): Powered by Gnani Timbre v2.5, producing studio-grade Indian language narration.
- Speech Recognition (STT): Powered by Gnani Prisma v2.5, supporting conversational multilingual voice input.
- Verified Facts & Safety: Answers are retrieved strictly from an administrator-vetted SQLite FTS5 knowledge base with verified dates and official ISRO citations.

Principles

  1. Light & Sovereign: Self-contained server architecture, local database, zero external tracker scripts.
  2. Honest Answers: Space Buddy never guesses or hallucinates dates. If a fact is unverified, it directs visitors to official publications.
  3. Accessible: Mobile-friendly, high audio clarity, and readable transcripts for all stories.
