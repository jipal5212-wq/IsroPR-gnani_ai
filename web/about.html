Mission & Public Outreach

Making Every Indian Citizen Space-Aware

Our Vision

From bicycle-carried rocket parts in Thumba to soft-landing near the lunar South Pole and observing the solar corona from the L1 Lagrange point, India's space program is a story of determination, scientific brilliance, and self-reliance.

IsroPR bridges the gap between complex orbital mechanics and every Indian citizen. By delivering spoken stories and voice-enabled conversational AI in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, English, and more, we ensure language is never a barrier to space awareness.

Technology & Voice Stack

Gnani Timbre v2.5 Studio-grade Indian language narration across Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, and English.
Gnani Prisma v2.5 Conversational multilingual speech recognition for voice notes and questions.
SQLite FTS5 RAG Administrator-vetted, verified ISRO facts with official citations and zero hallucinated dates.

Our Principles

  1. Light & Sovereign: Self-contained server architecture, local database, zero external tracker scripts.
  2. Honest Answers: Space Chat never guesses or hallucinates dates. If a fact is unverified, it directs visitors to official publications.
  3. Accessible: Mobile-friendly, high audio clarity, and immersive Spotify-style space podcasts.
